## Elemental relationship terminology

SpiroAnim uses a folk-community elemental model to describe timing-and-direction relationships.

Earth, Water, Air, and Fire are folk-community labels applied to VTG timing-and-direction
categories. They were not part of the original VTG terminology, although they have since received
some acknowledgment in contemporary VTG materials. Leonardo Icaza and Ronan McLoughlin embraced and
taught the model; its original creator remains unknown.

Sun and Moon extend the elemental model to quarter-time relationships. They were created by
[Austen Cloud](https://tkaflowarts.com).
